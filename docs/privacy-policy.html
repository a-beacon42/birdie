Birdie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

Birdie is a bird identification flashcard app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • No user accounts. Birdie does not require registration or login.
  • No personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information.
  • Game preferences. Your filter selections (region, card count, difficulty) are stored locally on your device only.
  • AI Chat. When you use the "Ask AI" feature, your messages are forwarded to Azure OpenAI to generate responses. Messages are not stored on our servers after the response is returned.

Third-Party Services

  • eBird API (Cornell Lab of Ornithology) — used for regional bird data and taxonomy.
  • Azure OpenAI (Microsoft) — used for the AI chat feature.
  • iNaturalist / Wikimedia Commons — bird photos sourced under Creative Commons licenses.
  • Xeno-canto — bird audio recordings under Creative Commons licenses.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

Birdie may collect anonymous crash reports to improve app stability. No personal data is included in these reports.

Data Storage

All game state is stored locally on your device. No user data is stored on our servers.

Children's Privacy

Birdie is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
